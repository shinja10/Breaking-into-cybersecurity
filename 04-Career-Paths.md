# 04 – Career Paths in Cybersecurity🕵️‍♂️💻

Cybersecurity is a vast field with many specializations. Below are common career paths, what they involve, and how to get started.

---

## 1. **Red Teamer (Offensive Security / Ethical Hacker)**
- **Role:** Simulate real‑world attacks to identify weaknesses before attackers do.
- **Skills:** Penetration testing, exploitation, privilege escalation, social engineering.
- **Tools:** Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Cobalt Strike.
- **Resources:**
  - [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com)
  - [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com)
  - [OverTheWire](https://overthewire.org)
  - [Metasploit Unleashed](https://www.offensive-security.com/metasploit-unleashed/)

---

## 2. **Blue Teamer (Defensive Security / SOC Analyst)**
- **Role:** Defend systems, detect attacks, and respond to security incidents.
- **Skills:** Log analysis, SIEM, threat detection, incident response.
- **Tools:** Splunk, ELK, Snort, pfSense.
- **Resources:**
  - [Snort Documentation](https://www.snort.org/documents)
  - [IDS vs IPS Explained](https://medium.com/@blue_team/ids-vs-ips-explained-9c6a23c4fdb1)
  - [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/)

---

## 3. **Purple Teamer**
- **Role:** Bridge Red and Blue teams to improve detection and response using attack simulations.
- **Skills:** Threat modeling, detection engineering, adversary emulation.
- **Tools:** MITRE ATT&CK, SIEMs, red‑team frameworks.
- **Resources:**
  - [MITRE ATT&CK Framework](https://attack.mitre.org/)
  - [Purple Teaming Explained](https://www.sans.org/blog/purple-team/)
  - [Detection Engineering Guide](https://www.elastic.co/security-labs/detection-engineering)

---

## 4. **Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker**
- **Role:** Test applications, networks, APIs, and infrastructure for vulnerabilities.
- **Skills:** Web, API, mobile security testing, scripting.
- **Resources:**
  - [PortSwigger Web & API Labs](https://portswigger.net/web-security)
  - [OWASP API Security Labs](https://github.com/OWASP/API-Security)
  - [Mobile Security Testing Guide](https://mobile-security.gitbook.io/)

---

## 5. **Network Security Engineer**
- **Role:** Secure and monitor networks, firewalls, and traffic.
- **Skills:** Networking, VPNs, packet analysis, firewall configuration.
- **Resources:**
  - [Nmap Official Manual](https://nmap.org/book/man.html)
  - [Wireshark Labs](https://www.wireshark.org/labs/)
  - [Linux Networking Commands](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4uKZ9sAqXc)

---

## 6. **Incident Responder**
- **Role:** Handle security breaches, contain threats, and lead recovery efforts.
- **Skills:** Forensics, malware analysis, crisis response.
- **Resources:**
  - [SANS Incident Response](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/incident-response/)
  - [DFIR Resources](https://www.dfir.training/)

---

## 7. **Digital Forensics Investigator**
- **Role:** Investigate cybercrimes and collect digital evidence.
- **Skills:** Disk, memory, and mobile forensics; report writing.
- **Tools:** Autopsy, Volatility.
- **Resources:**
  - [Autopsy](https://www.sleuthkit.org/autopsy/)
  - [Volatility Framework](https://www.volatilityfoundation.org/)

---

## 8. **Security Auditor / Compliance Specialist (ISO 27001, PCI‑DSS)**
- **Role:** Assess compliance with security standards and regulatory frameworks.
- **Skills:** Risk assessment, audits, policy review.
- **Frameworks:** ISO 27001, PCI‑DSS, NIST.
- **Resources:**
  - [ISO 27001](https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html)
  - [PCI‑DSS](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/)

---

## 9. **Cybersecurity Consultant**
- **Role:** Advise organizations on security posture, compliance, and risk.
- **Skills:** Technical + business communication, governance.
- **Certifications:** CISSP, CISM, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor.

---

## 10. **Cyber Law Lawyer / Legal Expert**
- **Role:** Handle cybercrime cases, data privacy, and digital compliance.
- **Skills:** Cyber laws, GDPR, IT Act, digital evidence.
- **Resources:**
  - [GDPR Info](https://gdpr-info.eu/)
  - [Cyber Laws (India)](https://meity.gov.in/content/cyber-laws)

---

> 💡 **Tip:**  
> - **Red Team** = Attack  
> - **Blue Team** = Defend  
> - **Purple Team** = Improve both  
>  
> Many professionals start in Blue Team roles and later move into Red or Purple teams.
